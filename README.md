![Genre](https://img.shields.io/badge/Genre-Legal%20Drama-blue)

## About the Series
**Matlock** is an American legal drama about a sharp, folksy defense attorney who takes on difficult cases and wins them by catching the truth in the details. The original series ran from *1986 to 1995* and starred Andy Griffith as Benjamin "Ben" Matlock. A new version premiered in *2024* with Kathy Bates in the lead role.

## Why I Like It
- Every episode is a self-contained courtroom mystery
- The lead character is smart, funny, and always one step ahead
- The cases reward paying attention to small clues
- It is easy to watch one episode at a time

## What Makes a Matlock Episode
1. A client is accused of a crime
2. Matlock takes the case and starts investigating
3. Clues are gathered and witnesses are questioned
4. A courtroom showdown exposes the real culprit

> In Matlock, the truth usually comes out in the courtroom.
